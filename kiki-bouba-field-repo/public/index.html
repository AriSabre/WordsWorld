Kiki Bouba Field

Two species of points drift around you. Type a real word and it is filtered, not drawn: its sound decides kiki (solid planes, built by the deep teal species) or bouba (openings with depth, lined by the olive species). Letter shapes set orientation, length sets size and thickness, syllables set repetition and typing speed sets how calmly the points settle.

Planes join only at 90° or 45°; right-angle joins cycle through all three axes, so walls turn corners and then roofs close over them. Soft openings grow organic. A faint pale stream threads every opening you can walk through. Nonsense makes the field flush red.
